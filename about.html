A little about us

More than just a hair appointment.

We like to keep things easy-going at Sophie Graham Hair. Whether you know exactly what you want or have a whole camera roll of inspiration to talk through, there's always room to ask questions, swap ideas and find something that works for you.

From perfectly blended balayage and bold, creative colour to beautiful cutting and styling, the team cares about how your hair looks and how you feel when you leave.
